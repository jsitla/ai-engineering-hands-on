# Northwind Home: customer handbook

Northwind Home is a made-up online shop used in the AI Engineering: Hands-On series. Everything in this handbook is fictional.

## Delivery

We deliver to Slovenia, Croatia, Austria and Italy. Orders placed before 13:00 on a working day leave our warehouse in Celje the same day. Delivery usually takes 3 to 5 working days. Parcels to islands in Croatia can take up to 2 extra days.

Shipping costs 4.90 euros per order. Shipping is free for orders over 50 euros. Large items, such as sofas and wardrobes, are delivered by a furniture courier for a flat fee of 39 euros, and the courier will call you the day before to agree on a time.

If a parcel has not arrived after 10 working days, contact customer service and we will start a search with the carrier. If the parcel is lost, we send a replacement or refund you in full.

## Returns and refunds

You can return any product within 30 days of delivery, without giving a reason. The product must be unused and in its original packaging. Custom-made items, such as curtains cut to size, cannot be returned.

To start a return, open your order in your account and choose Return items. You will get a return label by email. Returns from Slovenia are free. For other countries, the return shipping cost of 6.90 euros is deducted from your refund.

We refund the money within 14 days after the returned product reaches our warehouse. The refund goes back to the payment method you used for the order.

## Damaged or faulty products

If a product arrives damaged, take a photo of the damage and of the packaging, and report it within 14 days of delivery. We will send a free replacement or give you a full refund, and you do not have to pay for the return.

All electrical products have a 2-year warranty. If a product stops working within the warranty period, contact customer service with your order number. We repair it, replace it, or refund it. The warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, such as dropping a lamp.

## Payment

You can pay by credit or debit card, PayPal or bank transfer. For bank transfers, your order is reserved for 5 days and ships when the payment arrives. Business customers can ask for an invoice with their company's VAT number in the account settings before placing the order.

We do not offer payment in instalments. Prices on the website include VAT.

## Gift cards and discounts

Gift cards are available from 20 to 500 euros and are valid for 2 years from the date of purchase. A gift card can be used for several orders until the balance is used up. Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash.

Only one discount code can be used per order. Discount codes cannot be combined with sale prices, unless the code says so.

## Your account

To create an account, you need an email address and a password with at least 10 characters. If you forget your password, use the Forgot password link on the login page, and we will email you a reset link that is valid for 1 hour.

You can delete your account at any time in the account settings. We then delete your personal data within 30 days, except invoices, which we must keep for 10 years by law.

## Customer service

Our customer service team answers emails and chat messages from Monday to Friday, from 8:00 to 16:00. On Saturdays, the chat is open from 9:00 to 12:00. We answer most emails within one working day.

You can also visit our showroom in Celje, at Ljubljanska cesta 12, from Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00 to 18:00. Products in the showroom cannot be bought on the spot; please order them online.
